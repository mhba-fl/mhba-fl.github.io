How to Get There

By Car

Accessible via the Tampines Expressway (TPE). As this is a car-lite district, limited parking lots are available at SIT Punggol Campus. Alternative parking is available at SAFRA Punggol.

By Train/MRT

Alight at the Punggol Coast MRT station (NE18) along the North East Line (NEL),

or

Alight at Teck Lee LRT station (PW2), take Exit A and take a 15 mins walk under the sheltered walkways.

Directions to SIT Punggol Campus

SIT Punggol Campus is located in the north east part of Singapore, within the Punggol Digital District.

Address: 1 Punggol Coast Road, Singapore 828608
