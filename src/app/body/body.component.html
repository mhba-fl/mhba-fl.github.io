Featured Presentation: Frailty Management in Singapore: From Research to Real-World Implementation

Abstract:

Frailty affects approximately 3.5% to 27% of community-dwelling older adults in the Asia-Pacific region, increasing the risk of falls and significantly impacting physical health, psychosocial well-being, and community participation. These challenges place a growing burden on families and healthcare systems. This session will explore frailty in-depth, highlighting its risk factors and the standardized outcome measures used in both research and clinical practice. Attendees will gain valuable insights into the latest evidence-based frailty management strategies, including a newly developed multicomponent program that has demonstrated positive effects on health outcomes and fall reduction. The session will conclude by examining the role of AI technology in frailty management, paving the way for more inclusive, scalable, and effective interventions in the future.

Featured Presentation: AI applications in healthcare and diagnostic imaging

Abstract:

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into medical imaging has revolutionized the field of healthcare, offering unprecedented advancements in diagnosis and disease management. This talk will explore the latest innovations and applications of AI in medical imaging, highlighting its profound impact on patient care. AI technologies, such as deep learning algorithms, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), and generative adversarial networks (GANs), have significantly enhanced the accuracy and efficiency of medical image analysis. These advancements enable rapid and precise detection of abnormalities, from identifying tumors in radiological examinations to detecting early signs of eye disease in retinal images. In radiology, AI assists in reading X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs, pointing out potential issues that require further examination. Predictive analytics powered by AI uses historical imaging data to forecast disease progression, aiding in the creation of personalized treatment plans. Additionally, AI facilitates the integration of imaging data with electronic health records, providing a comprehensive view of patient health. Cardiovascular imaging has also benefited from AI, with applications across echocardiography, cardiac computed tomography (CCT), and cardiovascular magnetic resonance (CMR). AI improves patient selection, reduces image acquisition time, and enhances image optimization, ultimately improving diagnostic accuracy and patient outcomes. This talk will delve into some applications, showcasing how AI-based diagnostic tools not only speed up the interpretation of complex images but also improve early disease detection and personalized treatment planning. By embracing these cutting-edge technologies, healthcare providers can deliver better outcomes for patients, marking a paradigm shift in medical imaging and disease management.

Featured Presentation: Enhancing Elderly Healthcare: A Higher Education Initiative for Community-Driven ICT Solutions in Physical Monitoring and Emotional Well-being

Abstract:

This project aims to understand the specific needs of caregivers and elderly individuals, develop an open-source ICT architecture, and facilitate community-driven development. By improving elderly care through technology, this research seeks to address the growing challenge of aging populations in ASEAN countries and Japan, ensuring better health outcomes and quality of life for elderly citizens. Several ASEAN countries, along with Japan, are experiencing significant challenges related to their aging populations, necessitating urgent and effective policy and healthcare interventions. For instance, Japan saw 28.4% of its population aged 65 or older in 2020, with projections indicating this will rise to 38.1% by 20501. Similarly, Singapore's elderly population is anticipated to grow from 15.2% in 2020 to 23.8% by 20302. Other nations like Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, and the Philippines are also witnessing rapid increases in their elderly demographics3. This demographic shift poses considerable risks and challenges to social and economic frameworks. Aging populations exacerbate the strain on healthcare systems, heightening the need for medical services, long-term care, and increased healthcare expenditures. The World Health Organization (WHO) predicts a substantial rise in healthcare costs due to the prevalence of chronic illnesses and the need for continuous medical care among the elderly4. Additionally, the economic impact extends to labor markets, with a shrinking workforce and increased dependency ratios affecting productivity and economic growth5. Elderly individuals are at a higher risk of injuries, such as falls, which can lead to costly hospitalizations and long-term rehabilitation. Alarming statistics indicate that the cost of fall-related injuries among the elderly is escalating, posing a significant economic burden on families and healthcare systems6. Moreover, the societal impacts, including the emotional toll on caregivers and the broader community, necessitate comprehensive solutions to support both the elderly and their caregivers.

Abstract:

Featured Presentation: Eye Strokes: An Eye-gaze Drawing System for Mandarin Characters

Abstract:

In Singapore, 61% of elderly residents are English illiterate, necessitating communication alternatives for non-English literate Motor Neuron Disease (MND) patients who cannot speak or write. Existing eye-tracking solutions often use Mandarin pinyin which is unfamiliar to many older Singaporean Mandarin speakers, rendering these solutions ineffective. We investigated "Eye Strokes", a technique using eye gaze to "draw" Mandarin characters. This method aims to predict Mandarin characters for MND patients through their eye strokes drawn on our system to allow them to communicate better.

Advancing Medical Computer Vision Through Teacher-Student Learning and Privacy-Preserved Deep Learning Models

Abstract:

This talk introduces novel technical approaches for enhancing AI systems in medical imaging and human motion analysis. At the core of our methodology is an innovative teacher-student architecture that enables effective semi-supervised learning through cross-supervision and multi-scale cross contrastive learning. We demonstrate how this framework optimally leverages both labeled and unlabeled data in medical image segmentation, while introducing certainty-guided mechanisms to improve feature representation. For efficient model deployment, we present a novel knowledge distillation approach using global filters, which significantly reduces computational complexity while maintaining high accuracy in human pose estimation. Finally, our advanced privacy preservation framework that integrates differential privacy with integrated decision gradients, provides robust protection against membership inference attacks while preserving model utility. Through extensive empirical evaluation, we demonstrate how these technical innovations enable the development of lightweight, privacy-aware AI models suitable for real-world clinical deployment.
